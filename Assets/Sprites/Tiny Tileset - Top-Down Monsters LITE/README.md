## Attacks

| Monster | Attack 1 | Attack 2 | Death |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slime | Body Slam | Acid Spit | melts into a puddle |
| Skeleton (sword + shield) | Slash | Shield Bash | collapse + spirit |

## Specs
- 32 x 32 px frames, ground line at y = 29, centred on x = 16
- 4 directions: down, up, right, left
- 6 animations per direction (24 tags per monster): idle 4 · move 6 · attack1 6 · attack2 6 · hurt 3 · death 8
- the last death frame is the corpse/remains; hold it
- Soft drop shadow on its own layer in the `.aseprite` files
- 264 frames in total

## Folders
- `aseprite/`: `.aseprite` source per monster (tags such as `attack2_left`)
- `spritesheets/<monster>.png`: full sheet, one row per tag. `<monster>.json` holds frames, durations and tags
- `spritesheets/strips/`: one horizontal strip per animation
- `gifs/`: 4x previews
- `previews/`: promo GIFs (cover + showcase). Feel free to use them when you show your game or credit the pack.
