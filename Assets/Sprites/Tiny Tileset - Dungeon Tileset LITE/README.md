# Dungeon Tileset LITE (FREE) v1

Thanks for downloading! 🏰

This free pack has the **basics of a 32x32 top-down dungeon**: stone floors, a full set of walls, a few pieces of decor, an **animated wall torch**, an **animated treasure chest**, and two pickups: a **spinning coin** and a **health potion**. It's a free sample of the full **Dungeon Tileset**, made to match the Tiny Tileset characters.

- 🛒 **Get the full pack (120 tiles, 45 animated props, .aseprite sources):** https://tinytileset.itch.io/top-down-dungeon-tileset
- 🏰 **More asset packs from Tiny Tileset:** https://tinytileset.itch.io
- ⭐ A rating or comment on the itch page helps a lot. Follow the store to hear about new packs!

## 🧱 Tiles (`tiles/dungeon_tiles_lite.png`, 32x32 grid, 28 tiles)
`tiles/dungeon_tiles_lite_map.txt` lists every tile name with its column,row.
- **Floors:** 4 stone floors, 1 cracked floor, stairs down, floor shadow overlay
- **Wall tops:** the full 3x3 autotile set + 4 inner corners
- **Wall faces:** 3 brick variants, left end, right end
- **Decor:** barrel, crate, pot

Build rooms with **wall tops** around the edge, a row of **wall faces** along the top wall, then **floors**, with the floor shadow under the wall faces.
